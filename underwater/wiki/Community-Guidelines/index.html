Holodeck Community Guidelines

Our goal is to build and maintain a virtual enviroment that can advance AI research around the world. As such, we want to develop a strong, healthy community that can work together to build the Holodeck and its front end.

Seek to learn

No matter how much experience we have, we can always learn from those around us. By always striving to continue learning, collaborators will grow together, improving themselves and our product.

Be open-minded

Suggestions and advice on how to improve your solution should always be rightfully considered. Don't be afraid to suggest a refactor and give a reason to do so!

Keep it clean

No profanities or vulgarities.

Keep it civil

Hateful speech towards any other person or group of people will be removed. Personal attacks will not be tolerated. We should be sensitive and tactful when discussing any delicate topics.
